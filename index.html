Understanding the views

Ultrasound shows thin slices through the body. A clip sweeps through slices or records movement; it is not necessarily a fixed view of one structure.

Bright outlines often come from tissue boundaries or bone; fluid commonly looks dark. Crosses and ellipses are measurement markers. Colored overlays are ultrasound processing, not the natural color of the tissues.

Added titles use the original scan labels where available. Uncertain identifications are qualified. These descriptions do not assess whether a finding is normal. Some original labels or values are hidden in the public copies.
