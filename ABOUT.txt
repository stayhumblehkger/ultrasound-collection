ULTRASOUND COLLECTION — PUBLIC VIEWING COPY

This collection contains 158 ultrasound stills, 35 complete cine clips, and two informational cards for worksheets kept offline. Three studies are presented without examination dates.

Identifying headers are permanently covered in the image pixels and every video frame. Personal names, dates, record identifiers, facility/operator headers, calculated dating/measurement panels, and a sex-related text annotation have been removed. The two demographic-heavy worksheets, original clinical reports, DICOM metadata, source identifiers, original checksums, access links, and credentials are not included. Public descriptions omit exact patient-specific measurement values.

This is a viewing copy. Videos are re-encoded as H.264 MP4 at their original frame counts, dimensions, and playback rates; stills are freshly encoded JPEGs. Privacy masks obscure parts of the original images. The original full-resolution archive and reports remain offline.

Each image and clip has an AI-added title and plain-language description. The descriptions use visible source labels and appearance, with uncertainty where applicable. Every still and sampled frames from every clip were visually reviewed. Descriptions are a viewing guide, not a medical interpretation or assessment of normality. The original clinician's report remains the clinical reference.

The site contains no analytics, advertising, external fonts, or automatic calls to a hospital service. Optional medical-reference links open separately without a referrer. The site asks search engines not to index it; this is a request, not access control. Anyone with the public URL can view or copy this collection, and its hosting account can identify the owner.
